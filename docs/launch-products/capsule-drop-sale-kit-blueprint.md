# Capsule Drop Sale Kit Product Blueprint

This is the first product blueprint for turning the 3D story direction into a sellable 3S product.

The goal is not to sell a pretty image. The goal is to sell a complete premium campaign system for a boutique owner who needs urgency without making the brand look cheap.

## Current Evidence Level

- Confirmed: the product belongs under Fashion and Ecommerce.
- Confirmed: designs can be sold more than once; this is a non-exclusive digital product model.
- Confirmed: the visual direction should be story-led and premium, not a generic template catalog.
- Confirmed: the approved 3D direction is `docs/launch-products/storyboards/capsule-drop-sale-kit-3d-storyboard-v3-clean.png`.
- Likely: the first customer segment should be premium boutique owners and ecommerce sellers, not large enterprise fashion brands.
- Assumption: the delivery format should be Hybrid: Figma master plus Canva editable copy plus export package.

## Product Identity

Working title: `Capsule Drop Sale Kit`

Catalog title option:

- Capsule Drop Sale Kit

Subtitle:

- A premium sale campaign system for boutique drops, selected edits, and limited-time offers.

Core promise:

- Turn a sale into a private shopping moment that keeps the brand premium while still pushing customers to act.

Category:

- Fashion and Ecommerce

Suggested slug:

- `capsule-drop-sale-kit`

Relationship to existing starter product:

- This is the production-focused evolution of `Luxury Sale Without Noise`.

## Buyer

Primary buyer:

- Premium boutique owner.
- Small ecommerce brand founder.
- Fashion seller who runs limited drops or curated sales.

Secondary buyer:

- Freelance designer building campaign assets for fashion clients.
- Social media manager serving boutique brands.

Buyer pain:

- They need to sell fast without using cheap discount graphics.
- They do not have time to design a full campaign journey.
- Their sale posts often look disconnected across Instagram, stories, website, and reminders.
- They want customers to feel selected, not shouted at.

What the buyer is really buying:

- A ready-made campaign journey.
- A premium visual language.
- Less decision fatigue.
- A safer way to run a sale without damaging brand perception.

## Customer Story

Customer moment:

- A boutique is launching a small curated sale or collection drop and wants it to feel like a private edit, not a clearance event.

Before state:

- The products are premium, but the sale announcement looks generic, loud, or too similar to mass-market discount posts.

After state:

- The campaign feels selective, quiet, and intentional. Customers feel invited into a limited shopping moment.

Five-step scene sequence:

| Step | Scene                  | Business job            | Customer feeling                   |
| ---- | ---------------------- | ----------------------- | ---------------------------------- |
| 01   | Private announcement   | Introduce the sale/drop | Something exclusive is opening     |
| 02   | Product reveal story   | Show one hero item      | This edit has taste                |
| 03   | Website landing banner | Connect social to shop  | The brand is organized and serious |
| 04   | Product promo card     | Push the strongest item | I can see what is worth buying     |
| 05   | Final reminder         | Create urgency          | I should act before it closes      |

## Offer Stack

Minimum sellable package:

- 5 core editable templates.
- 2 product card preview images.
- 1 website hero preview image.
- 1 watermarked full preview.
- 1 README with usage instructions.
- 1 commercial license file.

Approved premium package:

- 30 editable templates total.
- 5 campaign stages.
- 3 channel families per stage.
- 2 visual variations per channel family.
- Figma master file.
- Canva editable copy.
- Exported PNG/WebP assets.
- Copy starter guide.
- Font and image license notes.

Decision:

- Launch v1 as the premium 30-template package.

Reason:

- This gives the product enough depth to feel like a full campaign system instead of a small template bundle.

## Premium 30-Template Matrix

Structure:

- 5 campaign stages.
- 3 channel families per stage.
- 2 visual variations per channel family.
- Total: 5 x 3 x 2 = 30 editable templates.

Channel families:

- Instagram post: square customer-facing announcement or promo.
- Instagram story: vertical mobile-first reveal, CTA, or reminder.
- Website/banner: ecommerce or landing-page placement.

Variation logic:

- Variation A: image-led and editorial.
- Variation B: type-led and offer-led.

| Stage                   | Channel family  | Variation A                             | Variation B                            |
| ----------------------- | --------------- | --------------------------------------- | -------------------------------------- |
| 01 Private announcement | Instagram post  | `CDSK / 01 / Post / Editorial Invite`   | `CDSK / 01 / Post / Offer Invite`      |
| 01 Private announcement | Instagram story | `CDSK / 01 / Story / Editorial Invite`  | `CDSK / 01 / Story / Offer Invite`     |
| 01 Private announcement | Website/banner  | `CDSK / 01 / Banner / Editorial Invite` | `CDSK / 01 / Banner / Offer Invite`    |

Size rules:

- Instagram post: 1080x1080.
- Instagram story: 1080x1920.
- Website/banner: 1600x900.

Design rule:

- Every stage must work as part of the journey and as a standalone asset.
- No variation should depend on fake rendered text inside preview images.
- All offer copy, dates, product names, and CTAs must remain editable.

## Visual Direction

Approved direction:

- Quiet luxury fashion studio.
- Black marble.
- Smoked acrylic.
- Ivory template cards.
- Champagne gold details.
- Taupe fabric and boutique product props.

Avoid:

- Loud discount stickers.
- Red/yellow sale graphics.
- Generic marketplace template look.
- Heavy serif wedding style.
- Overfilled cards.
- Any bottom feature strip inside the story image.

Current storyboard:

- `docs/launch-products/storyboards/capsule-drop-sale-kit-3d-storyboard-v3-clean.png`

Use this storyboard as:

- Product story reference.
- Website section direction.
- Figma mood reference.

Do not use it yet as:

- Final product preview.
- Catalog image.
- Downloadable customer asset.

The product page should not only show a gallery. It should sell the journey.

1. Product hero with the 3D story preview.
2. One-sentence promise.
3. The five-step campaign journey.
4. What is inside the package.
5. Editable platforms: Figma and Canva.
6. Use cases: capsule drop, private sale, limited edit, final reminder.
7. License clarity: non-exclusive commercial use.
8. Preview gallery with watermarked images.
9. FAQ about editing, resale, exclusivity, and file delivery.

Key page rule:

- The buyer must understand what they will receive before checkout.

## Pricing And License

Suggested base price:

- USD 49 for the first premium launch.

Reason:

- Existing draft price is USD 36.
- A 30-template premium package can justify USD 49 if the previews and delivery ZIP are complete.
- USD 36 can remain a future smaller tier if 3S wants a lightweight package later.

License model:

- Non-exclusive commercial license.
- Buyer can use the templates for their own business or client campaign.
- Buyer cannot resell the source files as templates.
- Buyer cannot claim exclusive ownership.
- Custom exclusivity is a separate service, not this product.

Important:

- Because 3S can sell each design more than once, the product page must not imply one-of-one ownership.
