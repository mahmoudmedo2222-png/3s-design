# Starter Five Launch Products

These are the first five 3S products: one from each launch category. They are prepared as product briefs and metadata drafts, not publishable products yet. They need real Figma/Canva files, previews, and delivery ZIPs before going live.

## 1. Noir Dining Launch Kit

Category: Restaurants and cafes  
Buyer: Luxury restaurant, fine dining venue, premium hospitality brand  
Moment: A new restaurant is opening and needs a launch presence that feels expensive before customers book.

Customer feeling:

- Premium
- Cinematic
- Exclusive
- Confident

Product promise:

Noir Dining Launch Kit gives a restaurant a polished launch set for Instagram, stories, and menu reveal moments. It should make the restaurant feel like a place worth booking, not just another food page.

Design direction:

- Dark editorial layout.
- Black, gold, warm ivory.
- Big food or dining hero image.
- Minimal typography with strong spacing.
- Subtle 3S watermark on public preview only.

Required frames:

- Instagram post: 1080x1080.
- Instagram story: 1080x1920.
- Menu cover: 1414x2000 or A4.
- Website hero preview: 1600x1200.

## 2. Glow Clinic Launch Kit

Category: Beauty and wellness  
Buyer: Beauty clinic, skincare studio, spa service provider  
Moment: A premium service is being announced and customers need to feel safe, clean, and confident.

Customer feeling:

- Clean
- Safe
- Luxurious
- Soft

Product promise:

Glow Clinic Launch Kit helps a beauty clinic announce a service with trust and softness. It should feel medical enough to be credible and luxurious enough to justify a higher price.

Design direction:

- Clean white base with champagne and sage accents.
- Soft portrait/product image zones.
- Calm gradients only inside product surfaces, not as vague decoration.
- Clear service name, benefit, and booking callout.

Required frames:

- Carousel cover: 1080x1080.
- Carousel detail slide: 1080x1080.
- Offer post: 1080x1080.
- Story booking frame: 1080x1920.
- Website hero preview: 1600x1200.

## 3. Signature Property Carousel

Category: Real estate and property  
Buyer: Real estate agent, brokerage, property developer  
Moment: A premium property needs to be presented clearly enough for serious buyers.

Customer feeling:

- Confident
- Spacious
- High-ticket
- Trusted

Product promise:

Signature Property Carousel gives agents a premium listing presentation that makes a property feel high-value and easy to inspect. It should help the buyer compare location, rooms, amenities, and price without visual noise.

Design direction:

- Spacious white or charcoal layout.
- Deep green, steel blue, or muted gold accents.
- Large image windows.
- Dense but readable property facts.
- Quiet navigation labels across carousel slides.

Required frames:

- Carousel cover: 1080x1080.
- Property details slide: 1080x1080.
- Amenities slide: 1080x1080.
- Story teaser: 1080x1920.
- Website hero preview: 1600x1200.

## 4. Luxury Sale Without Noise

Category: Fashion and ecommerce  
Buyer: Boutique, ecommerce store, premium fashion seller  
Moment: The brand wants urgency without looking cheap or crowded.

Customer feeling:

- Urgent
- Elegant
- Selective
- Commercial

Product promise:

Luxury Sale Without Noise gives a store sale graphics that convert while preserving brand value. It should make the offer feel curated and limited, not like a discount dump.

Design direction:

- Cream, black, and one accent color.
- Clear offer hierarchy.
- Product image area must be generous.
- No loud sticker overload.
- Use restrained sale badges.

Required frames:

- Instagram post: 1080x1080.
- Story sale frame: 1080x1920.
- Website/banner: 1600x900.
- Product grid promo card: 1080x1080.
- Website hero preview: 1600x1200.

## 5. Elegant Wedding Invitation Suite

Category: Events and invitations  
Buyer: Wedding planner, couple, event designer  
Moment: A wedding or private event needs an invitation suite that feels ceremonial and timeless.

Customer feeling:

- Ceremonial
- Elegant
- Timeless
- Warm

Product promise:

Elegant Wedding Invitation Suite gives planners and couples a premium invitation set with matching digital story assets. It should feel personal and expensive without becoming over-decorated.

Design direction:

- Ivory, pearl, gold, and muted floral accents.
- Elegant serif typography.
- Strong whitespace.
- Optional monogram area.
- Print and digital versions.

Required frames:

- Invitation front: 1080x1620 or 5x7 ratio.
- RSVP card: 1080x1620 or 5x7 ratio.
- Story announcement: 1080x1920.
- Detail card/menu: 1080x1620.
- Website hero preview: 1600x1200.

## Shared Delivery Rules

Each product must later ship with:

- Watermarked public preview.
- Delivery ZIP.
- Figma source link.
- Canva editable link.
- Exported PNG/JPG files.
- README.txt.
- Commercial license text.
- Font and asset license notes.

## Build Order

Start in this order:

1. Noir Dining Launch Kit.
2. Glow Clinic Launch Kit.
3. Signature Property Carousel.
4. Luxury Sale Without Noise.
5. Elegant Wedding Invitation Suite.

This gives the homepage enough range to feel like a real premium marketplace: food, beauty, real estate, ecommerce, and events.
