Capsule Drop Sale Kit
3S Design delivery notes

What this package is:
A premium campaign system for boutique drops, selected edits, and limited-time ecommerce offers.
The goal is to make a sale feel like a private shopping moment, not a discount blast.

Included when final:
- Figma master link.
- Canva editable folder link.
- Exported PNG/WebP previews.
- Watermarked public preview images.
- Commercial license text.
- Font and image license notes.
- Usage guide for editing without damaging the premium direction.

Current production status:
- Stage 01 source masters exist.
- Figma master exists.
- Canva V2 folder has 6 items, but 1 item is a V1 fallback because Canva AI conversion hit the monthly plan limit.
- Final customer delivery must wait until the fallback is replaced or manually rebuilt in Canva.

Customer editing rules:
- Replace product images first.
- Keep the black, ivory, taupe, and champagne gold palette restrained.
- Do not add loud discount stickers.
- Keep sale windows, access codes, and CTAs short.
- Export at the listed sizes without stretching text or product images.

Support note:
If a purchased file does not open or the download is broken, the customer should contact support with the order number.
