# Story-Led Design System

Every 3S product must sell a customer moment, not just a visual style.

The customer should understand:

- Why this design exists.
- What problem or moment it solves.
- What their brand feels like before using it.
- What their customer feels after seeing it.
- Where the design appears in the real business journey.

## Story Structure

Each design direction needs these fields before preview production:

1. **Customer Moment**
   The real business situation that makes the buyer need the design now.

2. **Before State**
   The weak or ordinary state the buyer is trying to escape.

3. **After State**
   The feeling and commercial result the design creates.

4. **Scene Sequence**
   Three to five practical scenes where the design is used.

5. **Buyer Promise**
   One sentence that explains why this product is worth buying.

6. **Visual Proof**
   The exact frames/assets that prove the promise on the product page.

## Example: Capsule Drop Sale Kit

### Customer Moment

A premium boutique is launching a small curated sale or collection drop. The owner needs urgency, but does not want the brand to look cheap.

### Before State

The brand has products worth buying, but the sale announcement feels generic, loud, or too similar to discount-store visuals.

### After State

The campaign feels selective and private. Customers feel they are being invited into a curated edit, not pushed into a messy discount.

### Scene Sequence

- Scene 1: Private sale announcement post.
- Scene 2: Story reveal with one hero product and a quiet CTA.
- Scene 3: Website banner for the capsule edit.
- Scene 4: Product promo card for the strongest item.
- Scene 5: Final reminder that keeps urgency elegant.

### Buyer Promise

Turn a sale into a private shopping moment that protects the brand while still pushing customers to act.

### Visual Proof

- Product card preview must show the private sale announcement.
- Hero preview must show the website banner plus social frames.
- Watermarked preview must show enough real layout detail to prove the pack is usable.

## Approval Gate

A design direction is not ready for final images until the story is approved.

If the story is unclear, do not generate or attach final previews.
